Quick Key Quest

Master PC keyboard shortcuts through 8 progressive levels

How to Play

Levels 1, 2, 5, 6: Click the correct keys on the virtual keyboard to perform the shortcut shown. In Recall levels, the shortcut is hidden for 10 seconds — see if you can remember it!
Levels 3, 4, 7, 8: Real practice! Follow the on-screen task using your actual keyboard. Click "Start Timer" when ready, then "Done!" when finished. Hints are shown in some levels.
Scoring: 10 points per task, minus 1 point for every 10 seconds. Be fast and accurate!
